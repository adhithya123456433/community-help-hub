PLATFORM WORKFLOW

How CommunityHelp Hub Works

A transparent, structured process for volunteers and organizations.

STEP 1

Register & Create Profile

Volunteers register with their skills and location. NGOs register with their organization details and registration number.

STEP 2

Publish & Discover Tasks

NGOs publish social-service tasks. Volunteers explore opportunities using smart skill matching and location proximity.

STEP 3

Complete & Get Certified

After assignment and task completion, volunteers earn ranking points and receive downloadable, verified PDF certificates.
